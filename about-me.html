greta franco /
communication designer

Based in Milan, I am a Communication Designer currently studying at Politecnico di Milano.
My practice is a blend of several disciplines: Editorial Design, Digital Communication, and Visual Identity, driven by a constant exploration of new creative inputs and contemporary aesthetics.
I am interested in how visual languages evolve across different media — from the tactile quality of print to the dynamic possibilities of digital interfaces. Through research, experimentation, and attention to detail, I develop projects that balance conceptual rigor with a fresh, contemporary sensibility.
