900 nodi /
identity & visual systems

900 Nodi is an artisanal brand dedicated to the production of handcrafted dog collars and leashes. The name stems from the union of two significant references: Novecento (900), the owner's dog, and the theme of knots (nodi), a metaphor strength, craftsmanship, and the bond between pet and owner. These elements form the conceptual foundation of the brand's visual identity project.

The logo was developed from a stylized representation of a dog looping onto itself. This choice visually synthesizes the brand's core values: the durability of the materials, the length of the leashes, and the uniqueness of the name. The resulting graphic solution is essential and highly recognizable, functioning both as a primary mark and a narrative element.

Building on the logo design, a modular pattern was created featuring the dog in various positions. This visual tool was conceived to ensure versatility across different applications, from packaging materials to digital and social media content. A dedicated color palette was integrated into the pattern and the overall visual identity, further showcased through a series of custom stickers. These colors, selected for consistency and brand recognition, are reflected in the pattern through the leashes worn by the dogs, strengthening the unity of the visual system.

The project is completed with a comprehensive brand identity system: custom packaging, mockups, and renderings demonstrate the consistency and functionality of the design. The overall result gives 900 Nodi a distinctive identity that effectively communicates craftsmanship and reliability. The entire visual language adopts a playful and dynamic tone—less formal and more aligned with the nature of the product—emphasizing the variety of colors and weaves that define the brand's artisanal offerings.
