Desert Quack /
motion & animation

Desert Quack is an animated piece that follows a duck's journey through the desert, in search of
an oasis where it can finally swim. The project originated from a collection of traditional sketches created by each team member, combining diverse techniques. The landscapes are animated using motion graphics, while the duck is animated frame-by-frame, digitally drawn in Illustrator. This visual narrative merges the craftsmanship of traditional drawing with the innovation of digital animation.

Research
We conducted extensive research on Pinterest, curating a dedicated moodboard to gather visual references for animation styles, character aesthetics, and landscape techniques.

Brainstorming
Through a collective brainstorming session, we compiled all ideas and creative directions into a shared working document.

Project Planning
Once the final concept was selected, we outlined the storyboard, divided the scenes, and assigned specific tasks within the team. During this phase, we also defined the official color palette.

Digitalization
The original hand-drawn plates were scanned and imported into Photoshop. We meticulously separated every element onto distinct layers, exporting them individually to prepare for the animation process.

Frame by frame creation
Following a study of the subject's motion, we developed a rough frame-by-frame animation sketch in Procreate. This was then exported and used as a reference in Illustrator to vectorize the movement across multiple layers. Finally, the sequence was assembled and refined using the Photoshop Video Timeline.
